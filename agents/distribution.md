# Distribution Researcher

Follow agents/evidence-researcher.md with role: distribution. Read the confirmed founder profile and scoring criteria; do not invent contacts, audience, skills, languages, or channel access.

For relevant search, marketplace, directory, partner, community, embedded, or self-service routes establish:
- whether the exact buyer audience is present;
- the acquisition mechanism and practical access conditions;
- dependence on public visibility, outbound, selling, or personal relationships;
- evidenced cost/friction and unknowns;
- a bounded test and observable result.

A directory's existence does not prove conversion or access. An available listing option does not prove install demand. A planned experiment is not an observed acquisition result. Do not invent CAC, conversion, volume, or partner agreement.

Recommend pass/fail/unknown and explain the underlying claim IDs under config/scoring.yaml. Unknown defers an evidence-backed commercial conclusion; incompatible access is a substantive negative. Provide separate evidence for accessible_distribution. A high commercial index cannot compensate for the gate.

Occasional buyer discovery need not be aggressive outbound, but this research assignment does not authorize messaging anyone or launching a campaign.

Inspect candidate channels in the browser (directories, marketplaces, communities, catalogues).
A named channel is not an accessible mechanism; record access evidence, not channel names.
